# Session Architecture & Workout Catalog — Torres / del Moral System

## Source
Synthesized from Karate Classroom Episodes 1-6 and real training cycles. Cross-referenced with sports science research on motor learning, part-whole practice, and periodization.

---

## 1. THE SIX-PART SESSION STRUCTURE

Every training session follows six sequential parts (from EP.1):

| Part | Name | Purpose | Approx. Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up | Physical preparation | 10-15 min |
| 2 | Gym / S&C Training | Strength & conditioning | 20-40 min |
| 3 | Kihon | Fundamental technique drills | 10-20 min |
| 4 | Kata Warm-up / Kata Review | Light kata work to transition into technical mode | 15-25 min |
| 5 | **Technical Kata Training** | THE main work — one kata, deep focused practice | 45-60 min |
| 6 | **Kata Workout** | High-intensity work on remaining 3+ katas | 20-40 min |

### Priority Order
If time is limited, prioritize Part 5 (Technical Kata Training) above all others. This is the non-negotiable core of the session. Part 6 (Kata Workout) is the second priority — it ensures all competition katas receive attention every training day.

### Weekly Structure (Ariel's Standard Schedule from EP.5)
- **Monday:** Technical kata training + kata workout
- **Tuesday:** Technical kata training + kata workout
- **Wednesday:** Technical kata training + kata workout
- **Thursday:** Circuit kata training (replaces standard workout)
- **Friday:** Competition rounds (full kata simulations)

Each day features a DIFFERENT main kata for Part 5. With 5 competition katas across 5 days, every kata receives one full technical session per week plus additional touches through workouts.

## 2. PART 4 — KATA WARM-UP / REVIEW

### Purpose
Physically and mentally transition from general training (Parts 1-3) into kata-specific mode. Review katas that are NOT the day's main technical focus.

### Format from Real Training Cycles

**Standard Review (Pre-comp Istanbul):**
```
Kata: Kururunfa (warm-up/review kata)
1/3 (×1) → 10 sec rest
2/3 (×1) → 10 sec rest
3/3 (×1) → 30 sec rest
Repeat for 5 ROUNDS
```
- Low volume per round (3 reps)
- Moderate total volume across 5 rounds (15 reps)
- Gentle ramp-up: each third performed once, then cycle

**Extended Review (Post-Istanbul):**
```
Kata: Kururunfa
1/3 (×3) → 10 sec rest + 1 min
  + Full kata (×2) with 1 min rest between
2/3 (×3) → same structure
3/3 (×3) → same structure
```
- Higher volume: each third ×3 PLUS full kata ×2 per section
- This is a heavier review — used between competitions when rebuilding

**Light Review (Recovery Week):**
```
5 katas cycled through:
Each kata: 1/3 (×2) → 10 sec rest → through 3/3
30 sec rest when completed each kata → move to next
```
- Every kata gets touched but lightly (6 reps per kata)
- Recovery-appropriate volume

## 3. PART 5 — TECHNICAL KATA TRAINING

This is the most important part of training. ONE kata per day, deep focused work.

### High-Volume Format (6+ weeks from competition, from EP.1)
```
Break kata into 4 quarters
Each quarter: ×10 reps, 30 sec rest between reps
Full recovery between quarters: 2-3 minutes

Quarter 1 (×10, 30s rest) → 2-3 min recovery
Quarter 2 (×10, 30s rest) → 2-3 min recovery
Quarter 3 (×10, 30s rest) → 2-3 min recovery
Quarter 4 (×10, 30s rest)

Duration: ~45-60 minutes
VLI: Volume 40 | Load 10 | Intensity 2/5
```
**IMPORTANT:** This extreme volume (×10 per section) is done for ONE WEEK ONLY, at the start of the training block. Do not repeat for multiple consecutive weeks.

### Moderate Format with Half Katas (~5 weeks from competition, from EP.2)
```
Quarter 1 (×5, 30s rest between) → 2 min recovery
Quarter 2 (×5, 30s rest between) → 2 min recovery
HALF KATA (first half) ×3, 1 min rest between → 3 min FULL recovery
Quarter 3 (×5, 30s rest between) → 2 min recovery
Quarter 4 (×5, 30s rest between) → 2 min recovery
HALF KATA (second half) ×3, 1 min rest between

VLI: Volume 26 | Load 8 | Intensity 4-4.5/5
```

### Pre-Competition Format (from Pre-comp Istanbul)
```
Main kata: Anan Dai
1/4 (×3 "con math") → 10 sec rest
1/4 (×8) → 30 sec rest + 2 min
Repeat for 2/4, 3/4, 4/4
```
- "Con math" likely refers to working with specific mathematical/technical corrections (focused technical review)
- High reps on the section (×8) but preceded by ×3 technical focus reps
- Lower rest between technical reps (10 sec), standard rest for main reps

### Between-Competition Format (from Post-Istanbul)
```
Main kata: Anan Dai
1/4 (×4) → 30 sec rest + 2 min
  + Full kata (×1) → 3 min rest
2/4 (×4) → same
3/4 (×4) → same
4/4 (×4) → same

VLI: Volume 20 | Load 9 | Intensity moderate-high
```
- Each quarter practiced ×4 PLUS a full kata after each section
- The full kata integrations check that section work translates into flow

### Recovery Format (from Recovery Week)
```
Anan: 1/3 (×3) → 30 sec + 2 min → through 3/3
Main kata: 1/4 (×3) → through 3/4
  + 1/2 (×1)
8 min allocated per section

VLI: Volume ~10-13 | Load ~3-4 | Intensity low-moderate
```
- Significantly reduced volume
- Time-boxed sections (8 min each) rather than rep-based
- Halves included but minimal reps

### Coach Integration Point
During the 30-second rest between reps, the coach provides ONE specific correction using external focus language. This is the primary feedback delivery mechanism. The athlete receives feedback, processes it, and immediately applies it in the next rep.

## 4. PART 6 — KATA WORKOUT CATALOG

The kata workout is ALWAYS the final part of training. It serves three katas that are NOT the day's main technical kata. This ensures all 5 competition katas are trained every day.

### BASIC WORKOUTS (EP.3)

#### Workout B1: Quarter Kata
```
Pick 1/4 section of each kata (strongest OR weakest section)
5 reps per section: 50% → 100% → 50% → 100% → 100%
NO REST between the 5 reps
2 min rest between rounds
1-3 rounds per kata (more rounds = further from competition)
Apply to 3 different katas

Taper: 6 weeks out = 3 rounds → competition week = 1 round
```
**The 50/100 alternation pattern:** 50% means controlled, focused on stance precision, arm placement, perfect technique. 100% means all-out power, speed, and spirit. The contrast builds the ability to produce quality under different energy states.

#### Workout B2: EMOM (Every Minute On the Minute)
```
Pick 1/4 or smaller section of kata
Set timer running continuously
Start section at each minute mark
Rest = whatever time remains in the minute (typically ~25 sec)
6-10 rounds per kata
2 min rest between katas
3 katas total

Best use: Target the MOST DIFFICULT section of each kata
Note from Ariel: "We've done this with Jesus using half kata sections — Grace had no rest left"
```

#### Workout B3: Beginning Blast
```
Pick beginning section of kata (1/4 or 1/3 size)
Set timer for 8 minutes
Complete 2-6 reps within those 8 minutes
Self-paced within the time block
3 katas, 8 min each

Taper: 6 weeks out = 6 reps → closer to comp = 2 reps
```

**Suggested weekly rotation (from EP.3):**
- Day 1: Quarter Kata
- Day 2: EMOM
- Day 3: Beginning Blast
- Day 4: (Circuit Kata — see below)
- Day 5: Competition rounds

### ADVANCED WORKOUTS (EP.4)

#### Workout A1: Burpee Kata Endings
```
Focus: Last quarter (1/4) of kata — competition finish quality
Pattern per kata:
  5 burpees → last quarter → 10 sec rest
  5 burpees → last quarter → 15 sec rest
  5 burpees → last quarter → 20 sec rest
  5 burpees → last quarter
= 4 total reps, escalating rest
2 min rest between katas
3 katas total
```

#### Workout A2: Leg Day Kata
```
Break kata into 3 equal sections
Pattern (6 lines total, 2 per "focus section"):

Line 1: S1@50% → S2@50% → S3@100% → 30s rest
Line 2: S1@50% → S2@50% → S3@100% → 30s rest
Line 3: S1@50% → S2@100% → S3@50% → 30s rest
Line 4: S1@50% → S2@100% → S3@50% → 30s rest
Line 5: S1@100% → S2@50% → S3@50% → 30s rest
Line 6: S1@100% → S2@50% → S3@50%

3 min rest between katas. 3 katas total.
```
**Key insight:** Each section gets 2 reps at 100% power and 4 reps at 50%. The 50% sections still require full stances — "50%" refers to speed/power, NOT stance quality. This builds endurance while rotating the high-intensity focus across the full kata.

#### Workout A3: Upper Body Kata
```
Pick one 1/3 section of each kata (pre-planned the night before)
5 reps alternating format:
  Full body → Upper body → Full body → Upper body → Full body

"Full body" = normal performance with full stances, kicks, everything
"Upper body" = standing upright, no kicks, no deep stances
  → MAXIMUM upper body power, generating force without leg drive

Option A: No rest between reps (harder)
Option B: 15 sec rest between each rep
2 min rest between katas. 3 katas total.
```

### CIRCUIT KATA TRAINING (EP.5)

**When:** Every Thursday (replaces standard workout). HIIT format.

#### Equipment Required
1. Battle rope (medium weight)
2. Resistance band (heavy, around waist)
3. Two medicine balls (4+ kg each, matching weight)
4. Jump box (safe height)

Place stations around the dojo with space for kata between them.

#### Protocol (One Round)
```
STATION 1: Battle rope × 30 sec (target: 100-120+ strikes)
→ 30 sec rest → Kata Section 1 at full power
→ 30 sec rest

STATION 2: Medicine ball shikodachi slams (side to side shuffle, 3-4 tatamis) × 30 sec (target: 8-12+ slams)
→ 30 sec rest → Kata Section 2 at full power
→ 30 sec rest

STATION 3: Resistance band knee drives × 30 sec (target: 120-150+ reps)
→ 30 sec rest → Kata Section 3 at full power
→ 30 sec rest

STATION 4: Burpee box jumps × 30 sec (target: 8-12 burpees)
→ 30 sec rest → Kata Section 4 at full power

FULL RECOVERY: 2-3 minutes
```

#### Rounds: Different kata per round (or repeat if < 5 katas)

#### 6-Week Circuit Taper
| Week | Rounds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (6 weeks out) | 5 rounds | Maximum circuit volume |
| Week 2 | 5 rounds | |
| Week 3 | 4 rounds | Begin tapering |
| Week 4 | 4 rounds | |
| Week 5 | 3 rounds | Light circuit week |
| Week 6 (competition) | 0 | No circuit training |

#### Post-Circuit Finisher (from EP.5)
After completing all circuit rounds:
```
Pick 2 best katas
Each kata: Half kata × 3 at full power, 1 min rest between halves
5 min break between katas
Repeat for second kata
```

### WEIGHT VEST KATA TRAINING (EP.6)

**Vest Weight:** 10-15% of body weight

**When to Use (6-week cycle):**
| Week | Vest Usage |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | No vest |
| Week 2 | No vest |
| Week 3 | Vest 3× that week |
| Week 4 | Vest 2× that week |
| Week 5 (taper) | No vest |
| Week 6 (competition) | No vest |

**CRITICAL:** Always at the END of training. Never before technical work. Duration: 20-40 minutes per session.

#### Protocol
```
Pick 2 kata sections (1/4 size each)

For each section:
  CYCLE:
    3× with vest at full power (no rest between)
    Remove vest
    2× without vest (no rest between)
    1 min rest
  REPEAT CYCLE × 3
  5 min rest

Then:
  Full kata × 2 WITH vest (no rest between)
  Remove vest
  Full kata × 1 WITHOUT vest

Do this entire protocol for 2 katas
```

**The contrast effect:** After removing the vest, the athlete experiences enhanced speed and power — the body has adapted to the extra load and overcompensates when it's removed. This is the same principle used in sprint training with resistance sleds.

**Pre-comp Istanbul shows this in practice:** The kata workout used quarter kata format WITH vest: Ohandai 3/4, Suparinpei 2/3, Anan 3/4. Pattern: 50%, 100%, 50%, 100%, 100%. 2 rounds per kata.

## 5. WORKOUT SELECTION GUIDE

| Workout | Primary Benefit | Best Used When | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quarter Kata (B1) | Endurance + section quality | Standard training, any phase | Medium |
| EMOM (B2) | Time pressure, difficult sections | Working on weaknesses | Medium-High |
| Beginning Blast (B3) | Strong kata openings | Competition prep (openings score heavily) | Medium |
| Burpee Endings (A1) | Cardiovascular + finish quality | Pre-competition (endings score heavily) | High |
| Leg Day Kata (A2) | Lower body endurance | Base building, off-season | High |
| Upper Body Kata (A3) | Upper body power generation | Technique refinement | Medium-High |
| Circuit Kata (EP.5) | Full-body HIIT + technique under fatigue | Thursday (weekly), builds competition resilience | Very High |
| Weight Vest (EP.6) | Explosive power, contrast training | Weeks 3-4 of 6-week cycle only | Very High |

### Customization Principle (from EP.4)
"Don't just do what I'm telling you to do. Try to study it. Try to learn it. And try to create something special just for you and your students." — Ariel Torres

Coaches should:
- Add or remove rounds based on athlete capacity
- Mix workout types within a week
- Adjust rest periods to match athlete fitness level
- Choose sections strategically (weakest sections for improvement, strongest for confidence before competition)
